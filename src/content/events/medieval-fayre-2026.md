The Friends of Grace Dieu Priory celebrated our 20th Anniversary Medieval Fayre with great success, welcoming hundreds of visitors to the conserved ruins under glorious 25°C weather.

### Highlights of the Day
- **Living History (1485 Camp):** Living history re-enactment groups, including the Friends of the Thomas Grey Household and the Ferrers Household, brought history alive with period armour, camp cooking, and crafts.
- **Archery & Drill:** Archers practised in the field opposite, followed by thrilling combat demonstrations and a traditional Mummers play (St George and the Dragon).
- **Music & Choirs:** The Sing It Happy Choir delighted audiences with acoustic choral performances inside the cloisters.
- **Community Market:** Local artisan stalls offered honey from Mount St Bernard's Abbey, handmade jewellery, preserves, woodcrafts, and Viking goods.
- **Refreshments:** Barista coffee, artisan pizza, burgers, and ice creams served behind the Tudor kitchen ruins.

Special thanks to our volunteer parking crew and car park marshals in Rusty Mary's field, Leicester University, Charnwood Geopark, and The Remet Company, Shepshed.
