The de Verdun family were powerful medieval nobles whose influence spanned England, Ireland, and Wales. When no male heir survived to inherit the family’s vast estates, Roesia de Verdun took control and ensured the dynasty’s fortunes continued.

This special talk by historian Sarah Wolek took place beside Roesia’s tomb in St John the Baptist's Church, Belton. The presentation illuminated what the iconography of her tomb reveals about her life and achievements, earning praise from King Henry III for accomplishing what none of her forefathers could.
