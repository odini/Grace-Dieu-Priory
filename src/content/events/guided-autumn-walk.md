Join our resident local historians for an autumnal guided walk around the precinct of Grace Dieu Priory.

We will explore:
- The surviving domestic buildings, chapter house, and cloister garth.
- The remnants of the historic Charnwood Forest canal bed and the towering railway viaduct.
- The newly exposed 800-year-old boundary wall discovered by volunteer working parties.

Sensible walking boots recommended. Route includes uneven ground and woodland paths.
