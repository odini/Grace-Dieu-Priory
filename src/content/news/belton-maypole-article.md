*The following article appeared in the October 2025 edition of the Belton Maypole magazine:*

In 1995 Grace Dieu Priory was a pile of stone covered in ivy. A dedicated group of local residents came together to save what was left, and the **Grace Dieu Priory Charitable Trust** company was born. The Trust now owns the Priory and Rusty Mary's field.

Between 1996 and 2006, extensive conservation work was carried out and, after overcoming many setbacks, the Priory was opened free of charge to the public. The **Friends of Grace Dieu Priory** was formed and registered as a Charity (No. 1117921) to manage day-to-day tasks. Over the last 20 years, the Priory has hosted thousands of visitors at events organised solely by volunteers, with no direct funding from any statutory body.

### An Urgent Appeal for Volunteers
The founding committee is aging and struggling to maintain the necessary level of activity. The Priory is financially sound and we hope to build a new bridge from Rusty Mary's field into the Priory.

We are not going to lock the gates in the near future, but unless we can recruit new help, that risk looms.

If you have office and administrative skills, digital know-how, or a bit of muscle to help put up gazebos and marshal parking at events, please consider becoming a Friend. We would love to welcome more younger members. You would be made very welcome!
