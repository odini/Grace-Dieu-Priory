The Friends of Grace Dieu Priory would like to extend our warmest thanks to everyone who attended our 20th Anniversary Medieval Fayre, which proved to be a resounding success!

### A Sunlit Day of Pageantry
After a very hot spell, the 28th of June 2026 was forecast to be a pleasant **25°C**—just right for the Medieval Fayre. Having looked after the ancient monument for 20 years, we wanted to celebrate this milestone with an event the entire community could share free of charge. After months of preparation, during which the final two setup days saw temperatures soaring over **30°C**, pitch markings for traders and car parking in Rusty Mary's field were ready to welcome our guests.

At 7:00 AM, the gates opened and our first living history reenactment groups and market traders began to arrive. Before our eyes, the priory was transformed into a 1485 medieval encampment with period armouries, camp cooking, and traditional woodcrafts, while arrows took flight in the practice drills across the meadow.

### Choirs, Combat & Community Spirit
By 10:30 AM, the ruins were bustling with hundreds of visitors trying on replica medieval helmets, learning squire combat drills, and trying wood-turning on period pole-lathes. The **Sing It Happy Choir** delighted audiences with acoustic choral harmony inside the ruined cloisters. Refreshments were served behind the Tudor kitchen, and families gathered to watch the traditional Mummers play as St George vanquished the dragon.

### Sincere Thanks to Our Volunteers
Our volunteer car park marshals did a sterling job guiding visitors safely into spaces in Rusty Mary’s field throughout the day. We would like to thank:
- The fabulous reenactment households: **The Friends of the Thomas Grey Household** and **The Ferrers Household**.
- Our partners at **Charnwood Forest Geopark** and their hardworking volunteer crew.
- The **University of Leicester** archaeology team and **The Remet Company, Shepshed**.
- All volunteer marshals and gate crew who made the day run seamlessly.

Most of all, thank you to our community for coming along, supporting the event, and leaving the monument ruins so clean and tidy.
