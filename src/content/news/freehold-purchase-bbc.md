In November 2024, our parent organisation, **The Grace Dieu Priory Trust**, officially acquired the freehold of the entire site after two years of negotiations with private landowners.

Having previously cared for the monument as tenants, freehold ownership now provides long-term certainty and substantial flexibility to execute ambitious conservation work.

Planned initiatives include:
- Scheduled archaeological excavations across previously unexplored ground.
- Capital works to build a dedicated footbridge linking Rusty Mary's field to the priory precinct.
- Continued consolidation and restoration of delicate medieval stonework.
