In November 2024, our parent organisation, **The Grace Dieu Priory Trust**, officially acquired the freehold of the entire site after two years of detailed negotiations, securing permanent ownership for a peppercorn sum of £10.

As reported by **BBC News** (*"Leicestershire priory ruins sold by Catholic church in £10 deal"* by Isaac Ashe), the sale ends decades of tenant status and guarantees the site's permanent protection as a free-to-visit public heritage landmark.

Having maintained the ancient monument as tenants since the 1990s, freehold ownership provides long-term security and unlocks ambitious conservation initiatives:
- Scheduled archaeological excavations across previously unexplored ground.
- Capital works to construct a dedicated footbridge connecting Rusty Mary's field directly to the priory precinct.
- Continued consolidation and restoration of delicate 13th-century medieval stonework.
- Preservation of the secluded Charnwood Forest woodland parkland for future generations.
