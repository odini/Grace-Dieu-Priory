We would like to extend our immense gratitude to our friends and volunteers at **Charnwood Forest Geopark** for their outstanding hands-on support.

During joint conservation working parties focused on tidying visitor footpaths and clearing invasive brambles, volunteers successfully exposed an intact section of the priory's original 800-year-old medieval wall.

This discovery provides fresh insight into the monastic boundary architecture and will form a key focal point for upcoming educational guided walks.
