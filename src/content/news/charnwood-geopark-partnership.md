**IT'S OFFICIAL!** We are now official partners of the Charnwood Forest Geopark.

The team at the Geopark and their dedicated volunteer working parties have provided indispensable support over the past year. Grace Dieu Priory is honoured to stand among the region's premier historic and geological attractions as the landscape strives towards prestigious **UNESCO Global Geopark** status.

Discover more about the Geopartner network at [charnwoodforest.org](https://www.charnwoodforest.org/meet-the-geopartner-network/).
