A beautifully produced volume authored by renowned regional naturalists Steve Woodward and Helen Ikin of the **Loughborough Naturalists' Club**.

This comprehensive study documents the flora, fauna, lichens, and unique geological formations found within the Grace Dieu Priory estate, the Grace Dieu brook, and the surrounding Charnwood ancient woodlands.

Proceeds from the sale of this book directly support both The Loughborough Naturalists' Club and The Friends of Grace Dieu Priory.
